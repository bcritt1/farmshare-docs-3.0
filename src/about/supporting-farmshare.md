FarmShare is free to use and has limited dedicated funding. There are a few ways
to help it keep running and grow.

If you use FarmShare to teach a class, tell your department chair. If you're in
a registered student group that uses it, tell your faculty representative or the
ASSU. Knowing who relies on FarmShare helps make the case for supporting it.

If you'd like to contribute funds for more capacity, email
{{ facts.support_email }} with "FarmShare funds contribution" in the subject. We
can arrange an iJournals transfer to our PTA, or a capital purchase from yours.

If your group has hardware that SRC already supports, FarmShare may be able to
use it once you're done with it. Email us to ask.
