FarmShare's policies cover who can use it, what it can be used for, and what
data it can hold.

## Who Can Use FarmShare

Stanford students, faculty and staff with a full-service [SUNet
ID](https://uit.stanford.edu/service/accounts/sunetids) can use FarmShare. A
full-service ID is one that includes Stanford email. If you don't have one, for
example as a visiting researcher, someone with spending authority can sponsor
one for you, for a [monthly fee]({{ facts.sponsorship_rates_url }}) charged by
University IT.

## What It's For

FarmShare is for coursework, teaching and unsponsored research. It isn't for
sponsored or departmental research. If your work has a grant or other dedicated
funding, see [Sherlock](../resources/sherlock.md).

Using FarmShare means following the University's policies on acceptable use and
conduct, including:

- the [Administrative Guide](https://adminguide.stanford.edu/), especially the
  [University Code of
  Conduct](https://adminguide.stanford.edu/chapter-1/subchapter-1/policy-1-1-1)
  and the [Computer and Network Usage
  Policy](https://adminguide.stanford.edu/chapter-6/subchapter-2/policy-6-2-1)
- the [Research Policy
  Handbook](https://doresearch.stanford.edu/policies/research-policy-handbook)
- the [Honor
  Code](https://communitystandards.stanford.edu/policies-and-guidance/honor-code)
  and the [Fundamental
  Standard](https://communitystandards.stanford.edu/policies-and-guidance/fundamental-standard)

## Data Risk

FarmShare is approved for low- and moderate-risk data only. Don't store, process
or transmit [high-risk data](https://dataclass.stanford.edu/) on it, including
protected health information and personally identifiable information. For
high-risk data, see [Carina](../resources/carina.md) or
[Nero](../resources/nero.md).
