FarmShare is for coursework, teaching, and research that doesn't have its own
funding. It's set up like Stanford's larger research clusters, so what you learn
here carries over if your work later moves to one of them.

## Who Can Use FarmShare

Anyone with a full-service SUNet ID can use FarmShare. A full-service ID is one
that includes Stanford email, so if you have a `stanford.edu` email address, you
can log in. You don't need anyone's approval, and there's no charge.

Your access continues for as long as your SUNet ID stays active, so you don't
lose it when a course ends.

If you're a visiting researcher or another affiliate without a full-service ID,
someone at Stanford with spending authority can sponsor one for you. University
IT charges a [monthly fee]({{ facts.sponsorship_rates_url }}) for sponsored IDs.
Ask a faculty member or manager in your department about sponsorship.

## What FarmShare Is For

You can use FarmShare for coursework and class assignments, for teaching
(instructors can ask for shared course directories; see
[Teaching](../teaching/index.md)), and for your own research, as long as it
isn't funded by a grant or other dedicated source.

## What FarmShare Isn't For

FarmShare isn't meant for sponsored or departmental research. If your work has a
grant or other dedicated funding, [Sherlock](../resources/sherlock.md) is the
cluster for it.

FarmShare is approved for low- and moderate-risk data only. Don't use it to
store, process or transfer high-risk data, such as protected health information
or other personally identifiable information. See
[Policy](../reference/policies.md) for the details.

## Other Options

If FarmShare isn't the right fit, Stanford Research Computing runs other
systems:

| System | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [Sherlock](../resources/sherlock.md) | Sponsored and departmental research, larger jobs, group storage |
| [Carina](../resources/carina.md) | Research with high-risk data |
| [Nero](../resources/nero.md) | Cloud computing and big data work with high-risk data |
| [Marlowe](../resources/marlowe.md) | Large AI and GPU workloads |

When you're ready, [log in for the first time](first-login.md).
