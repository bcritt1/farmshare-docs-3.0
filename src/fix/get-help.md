You can get help from us by email, and from other FarmShare users on Slack. The
sections below say where to go and what to include in a request.

## Slack

FarmShare has two channels on Stanford's Slack. For how to sign in, see the
University IT [Slack page](https://uit.stanford.edu/service/slack).

- [`{{ facts.announce_channel }}`]({{ facts.announce_url }}) is a low-volume
  channel for outages, maintenance and other announcements. Check it first if
  FarmShare seems to be down.
- [`{{ facts.users_channel }}`]({{ facts.users_url }}) is where FarmShare users
  help each other.

## Email Support

<!-- NEEDS REVIEW: Does adding "urgent" to the subject change how a request is
handled? -->

Email {{ facts.support_email }} with "FarmShare" in the subject line. We answer
during business hours, so requests sent on a weekend are picked up on the next
business day. If something breaks close to a class or a deadline, add "urgent"
to the subject.

Include enough for us to see what happened without writing back:

1. Your SUNet ID.
2. What you were doing, and what you expected to happen.
3. The exact error message, copied as text rather than a screenshot.
4. The job ID, OnDemand session type or node name, if there is one.
5. Roughly when it happened.

## Accessibility

If you run into accessibility barriers on this site, visit [Digital
