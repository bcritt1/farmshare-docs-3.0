Sherlock is Stanford's large shared computing cluster for faculty and their
research groups. Stanford Research Computing runs it, like FarmShare, but it's a
separate system with its own accounts, storage and documentation.

## When to Use Sherlock Instead

FarmShare is for coursework and unsponsored research. Sherlock is the better fit
when:

- your research is sponsored, or funded by a grant or department
- your jobs need more than FarmShare's limits allow, such as more memory than a
  FarmShare job can use
- your group needs shared storage that lasts beyond a single course

Sherlock isn't meant for coursework or class assignments, so course projects
stay on FarmShare.

## Getting an Account

A faculty member sponsors Sherlock accounts for their group, so ask your advisor
or PI to request one for you. Any SUNet ID can be used.

See the [Sherlock documentation](https://www.sherlock.stanford.edu/docs/) to get
started.
