Marlowe is a large GPU cluster for AI and other research that needs specialized
hardware. Stanford Research Computing runs it, like FarmShare, but it's a
separate system with its own accounts and documentation.

## When to Use Marlowe Instead

FarmShare has {{ facts.gpu_nodes }} GPU nodes, and each person can use up to
{{ facts.qos.gpu.gpus }} GPUs at a time. That's enough for learning and for
smaller models. If your research needs many GPUs for large-scale AI work, look
at Marlowe.

## Getting Access

Access to Marlowe is through project applications. See the [Marlowe
documentation](https://docs.marlowe.stanford.edu/) for how to apply.
