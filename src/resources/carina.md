Carina is a secure computing environment approved for high-risk data. Stanford
Research Computing runs it, like FarmShare, but it's a separate system with its
own accounts and documentation.

## When to Use Carina Instead

FarmShare can't be used for high-risk data, such as protected health information
or personally identifiable information. If your work involves high-risk data,
use Carina or [Nero](nero.md). See Stanford's [data risk
classifications](https://dataclass.stanford.edu/) to check how your data is
classified.

## Getting Access

See the [Carina documentation](https://carinadocs.sites.stanford.edu/) for how
to request access.
